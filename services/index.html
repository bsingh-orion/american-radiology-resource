Services

From the outgoing system to the one that replaces it.

The trouble in a pre-owned deal is rarely the invoice. It is the rigging path, the power, the tube history, and the week the department cannot be dark.

Previously owned CT, MRI, PET/CT, cath and angio labs, and rad rooms. You get a specific system, its history as we know it, and a straight answer if it is the wrong fit.

If you are replacing a scanner, we will look at buying the outgoing system. A clean removal is often worth more than a hopeful listing that sits for a year.

Interim CT, MRI, and PET coaches on a short or longer term. The point is to keep the schedule while a room is built, repaired, or still being decided.

Rigging in and rigging out. Magnet ramps, gantry moves, and the unglamorous sequencing that keeps a department from being dark longer than it has to be.

Power, cooling, shielding conversations, pad, and access — reviewed against the system you are actually considering, not a generic room template.

Service coverage can be arranged around the system you take. We do not pretend a pre-owned scanner is maintenance-free, and we will not leave that as a surprise.

Applications orientation for the team that will run the system. Ask as well about interim staffing if a go-live and a vacancy land in the same week.

When a purchase makes more sense than a rental but cash this quarter does not, we can discuss financing alongside the equipment — not as a separate errand after the fact.

A rental is a project too.

A coach needs a pad, power, a place for patients to wait, and a date it can leave. We would rather decline a rental than park a trailer the site cannot support.
